## Per-Vendor Notes

### Angi Leads / HomeAdvisor (Angi Ads / mHelpDesk lineage)
- **Pricing model:** Per-shared-lead **plus** an annual membership (~$300/yr, [EST]) that sits on top of per-lead charges. Typically sold on a **12-month contract** with reported 30–35% early-cancellation penalties and 60-day notice [EST].
- **Shared vs exclusive:** Shared. Same lead is sold to **3–8 contractors** simultaneously [EST]. Angi also cross-distributes leads across its family brands (HomeAdvisor → CraftJack etc.).
- **Refund/credit policy:** Credits for invalid leads (wrong number, out-of-area, spam), **not cash refunds**; credits apply to future leads only, with per-territory dispute limits [EST].
- **Close-rate claims:** No official per-trade close rates found. Third-party sources cite shared-lead conversion ~13–20% vs exclusive ~27–30% (generic, not Angi-published) [EST]. One contractor anecdote: ~112 leads from a $2,500 spend (no close rate given).
- **2025 model change:** As of Jan 13, 2025, homeowners now actively pick which pros to match with (vs auto-distribution) — reported to lift lead quality [EST].
- **GATING:** Actual per-trade/per-ZIP pricing is NOT on a public page — it's shown only after signup/quote. All Angi trade numbers here are secondary-source estimates. **FLAGGED.**

### Networx
- **Pricing model:** Prepaid credit system. Two plans — **Pay Per Lead** (shared) and **Exclusive Leads**. Also offers "Direct Calls" for urgent work. You pre-fund a monthly budget; each accepted lead deducts its value.
- **Shared vs exclusive:** Pay Per Lead = shared, **up to 4 contractors total**, delivered real-time via SMS + email [OFFICIAL]. Exclusive plan = single-buyer, priced higher.
- **Published ranges [OFFICIAL]:** Pay Per Lead **$10–$100+**; Exclusive **$15–$120+**. Help center explicitly states exact per-category/per-area prices are only visible in the dashboard/app "Plan" section.
- **Refund/credit policy:** Not stated on the two help articles fetched (no public bad-lead credit policy located).
- **Close-rate claims:** None published by Networx.
- **GATING:** Only broad plan-level ranges are public; **per-trade pricing is gated behind the logged-in dashboard.** **FLAGGED** (partial gating — ranges are official, trade detail is not).

### CraftJack
- **Pricing model:** Pay-per-shared-lead with a signature **Speed-To-Call discount** — call the homeowner within 30 min of receipt and save ~20% on that lead (e.g., $67 kitchen-remodel lead → $53.60) [EST]. Contractors reportedly averaged ~17% savings when a discount applied.
- **Shared vs exclusive:** Shared — up to **3 contractors** per lead, national average ~1.5 [EST]. (CraftJack is an Angi subsidiary and participates in Angi's cross-brand lead sharing.)
- **Published ranges [EST]:** ~$20–$60/lead, varying by service package and location.
- **Refund/credit policy:** Historically credited bad leads (renters, fax numbers, wrong contacts) via the lead-management tool [EST].
- **Close-rate claims:** None published.
- **GATING (major):** As of this research, **the entire craftjack.com domain 301-redirects to `https://signup.angi.com/pro`** — the public pricing/toolbox pages (discountedleads, speed-to-call, updates-and-new-pricing) are gone, and BBB lists the entity as winding down/"out of business." CraftJack pricing is now effectively **fully gated behind Angi's pro signup.** All CraftJack numbers here are from cached/secondary sources and may be stale. **FLAGGED — HARD GATE.**

### Thumbtack
- **Pricing model:** **Pay-per-lead** (not the older pay-per-booking; Thumbtack moved back to charging when a customer reaches out — message, booking, or call). **No membership or annual fee** [OFFICIAL: "you only pay when a customer reaches out"]. Pros set a **max price per lead** and a **weekly budget**.
- **Dynamic pricing [OFFICIAL]:** Thumbtack states prices **vary by job type, job size, market, and competition** and are updated ~weekly by supply/demand. There is **no universal flat rate** — this is the load-bearing official statement; the dollar figures below are third-party estimates fit to it.
- **Shared vs exclusive:** Shared — each project is shown to **~4–5 matching pros**, all of whom can quote.
- **Refund/credit policy [OFFICIAL-ish]:** Thumbtack refunds/credits leads that don't meet its criteria (e.g., customer never intended to hire, spam, wrong category) — surfaced in help docs; exact terms not fully machine-readable on the JS-rendered help page.
- **Close-rate claims:** Marketing/secondary sources claim "78% of homeowners go with the first pro to respond" and that fast response lifts conversion from ~10–15% to ~25–35% [EST] — not an official per-trade close rate.
- **GATING note:** The official help page (help.thumbtack.com/article/pay-for-leads) is JS-rendered and returned empty to a plain fetch; it confirms the *model* ("varies") but **does not publish per-trade dollar figures** — so all trade-level Thumbtack numbers here are [EST]. **FLAGGED** (model is public; trade prices are not officially published).

## Synthesis — Where Our Reasoned $-Tiers Likely Sit vs Market

Market reality, per trade (blended across the four incumbents, shared leads):

| Trade | Rough market per-shared-lead band (blended [EST]) |
|---|---|
| Cleaning (house/janitorial) | ~$8–$35 |
| Landscaping | ~$10–$55 |
| Pest control | ~$18–$50 |
| Painting | ~$15–$60 |
| HVAC | ~$20–$100 (install skews high) |
| Roofing | ~$30–$150+ |

Takeaways for our pricing:
1. **Shared is the default; exclusive commands a premium.** Every incumbent's cheap tier is a shared lead (3–8 buyers on Angi, 4 on Networx, ~3 on CraftJack, 4–5 on Thumbtack). Exclusive pricing runs roughly 1.3–2x the shared price (Networx: $10–$100 shared vs $15–$120 exclusive). If we sell **exclusive** leads, we can price at the top of these bands and still undercut the *effective* cost of a shared lead (a $50 shared lead split 5 ways is a ~$250 effective cost per won job at ~20% close).
2. **Trade ordering is consistent:** cleaning < landscaping ≈ pest < painting < HVAC < roofing. Our tiers should mirror this ordering; roofing/HVAC support the highest $-tiers because job values are $5k–$25k.
3. **Where our reasoned tiers likely land:** if our tiers were set on job-value logic, expect them to be roughly market-aligned for HVAC/roofing (high) and possibly **under-priced for cleaning/pest** where incumbents still get $15–$50 for low-ticket work — room to raise the floor. Conversely, if our tiers exceed the roofing $150 ceiling, that's above the shared-market comp and only justifiable as exclusive.
4. **Membership/contract friction is our wedge.** Angi bundles a ~$300/yr fee + 12-month contract; Networx/CraftJack use prepaid budgets; Thumbtack is the most flexible (no fee, weekly budget, dynamic). A no-contract / no-membership, exclusive-lead offer is differentiated against the incumbents' friction.
